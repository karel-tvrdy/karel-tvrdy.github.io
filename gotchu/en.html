Gotchu

Know where your family is – without anyone being tracked.

Android and iPhone · currently in testing

"Where are they?" only on request

Gotchu is not a continuous tracker. It determines a phone's location only when someone in the group asks for it – and sends it only to the person who asked.

You control sharing

Location sharing is off by default. You turn it on yourself for a group you have invited each other to, and you can turn it off at any time.

See who looked for you

Every request for your location is logged. You can always check who looked for you and when.

Live tracking only with consent

When a journey needs to be followed, the tracked person has to confirm it on their phone. Live tracking is time-limited and either side can stop it at any time.

Parental supervision for children under 15

A parent can lock a child's location sharing and start live tracking without the child having to accept. Supervision is never secret – the child sees it and always knows who looked for them. It ends on its own at 15.

No ads, no selling of data

Your location is only for your group. We don't sell it, we don't share it with advertisers and the app contains no ads.
