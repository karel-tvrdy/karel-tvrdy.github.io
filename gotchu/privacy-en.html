Privacy Policy

Effective from 28 September 2026

1. Controller

The controller of your personal data is Karel Tvrdý, company ID (IČO) 05438497 (the "operator"). Contact: tvrdy.k@seznam.cz.

2. How the app works

Gotchu lets people who have invited each other into a group share their location. The app does not track you continuously – it only checks your location in the situations described below.

3. What data we process

  • Account data: email, password (stored only in encrypted form), the name you choose and optionally a photo or icon.
  • Groups: which groups you are in, your role, whether you share your location in them and invites you created.
  • Location: coordinates, their accuracy, time and your device's battery level – only (a) when a group member you share your location with asks for it ("Where are they?"), (b) during live tracking you confirmed yourself, and (c) once when your battery runs low (can be turned off in Settings).
  • A record of who asked for your location and when (visible in the app under "Who requested my location").
  • Nicknames and photos you set for other members (visible only to you).
  • Technical data: a device identifier for delivering notifications and your chosen app language.

4. Why and on what basis

We process your data to provide the service you chose (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Location sharing is based on your consent, which you give by turning on sharing in a group, confirming live tracking and keeping the "Capture location on low battery" option on (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent anytime by turning sharing off, leaving a group or turning the option off in Settings.

5. Who can see your data

  • Group members you share your location with – only locations found at their own request or during live tracking you allowed. A location captured on low battery is shown to them only when "Where are they?" can't reach you.
  • The operator, to the extent needed to run and support the service.
  • Processors providing technical operation: Supabase Inc. (database, sign-in, photo storage), Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging – notification delivery), OpenStreetMap Foundation (map tiles – your device downloads them from their servers when showing a map) and your device's operating system service for turning coordinates into an address (Google on Android).

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

6. Transfers outside the EU

Some processors (Google, Supabase) may process data outside the European Union. In that case it happens under GDPR safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), we delete your profile, locations, request records, memberships, invites, photos and groups where you were the only member. Database backups at the hosting provider may still contain the data for a limited time until they are overwritten.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to data portability, to object and to withdraw consent. You can do most of this directly in the app (editing your name and photo, turning sharing off, deleting your account); for anything else contact tvrdy.k@seznam.cz. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (www.uoou.cz) or your local data protection authority.

9. Age

The app is intended for people over 16.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will let you know about significant changes in the app.
